Website privacy

This notice covers quietspeech.app. The Mac app transcribes on-device by default and has no app telemetry. Optional external providers change where selected audio or text is processed.

Website analytics

We use Minilytics for website visits, campaign attribution and interactions such as checkout clicks. Campaign tags may be stored in your browser for 30 days and passed to Polar at checkout. A Minilytics visitor identifier can also be passed to Polar to match an order to its visit.

Optional Google advertising measurement

If you allow advertising measurement, we load Google’s tag to measure visits and purchases resulting from our ads. Google may receive information such as your IP address, device/browser information, page URL and ad-click identifiers. For a verified purchase, we send the transaction identifier, purchase value and currency. We do not provide your email address, name, payment details, dictation audio or transcripts to Google through this integration. Personalized-ad signals are disabled.

Declining keeps Google’s advertising tag from loading on this website. Checkout still works. Your preference is stored in your browser for up to 180 days. . After withdrawing permission, reload the page to unload a tag already running. Google’s handling of information is described in its partner-site privacy information.

Email yourself a Mac link

“Send me the link” opens a draft in your email app. You choose the recipient and send it yourself. This feature does not collect your email address or automatically send an email. The draft contains the QuietSpeech page link, Mac requirements and available campaign tags, so an order on your Mac can retain its original source. Ad-click, visitor and checkout identifiers are not included in that link.

Payments and purchase verification

Polar handles checkout, payment processing and license delivery under its privacy policy. Our server checks a checkout’s payment status with Polar before reporting a purchase. The verification response contains only a transaction identifier, amount and currency, and is not cached. The site keeps a short list of transaction identifiers in your browser to avoid duplicate measurement.

Your choices

You can decline advertising measurement, change the preference above, or clear this website’s browser storage. Browser privacy controls may also limit measurement. For questions, contact the maker.
