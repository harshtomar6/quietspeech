Before you switch

A few useful answers.

Does QuietSpeech need an API key?

No API key is needed for the default setup: Apple’s recognizer transcribes speech and Apple Intelligence handles local AI text work when available. External providers are optional.

Can I use QuietSpeech offline?

Yes, by default, once the required Apple speech assets are installed. Initial setup may need downloads. The license needs an internet check at least once every 14 days. External cloud providers require a connection.

Does it work without Apple Intelligence?

Basic dictation works with rules-based cleanup. Local AI polishing, selected-text editing and generation need Apple Intelligence. You can also configure another AI provider.

Is it more accurate than Wispr Flow?

We have not published a controlled comparison. QuietSpeech uses Apple’s recognizer by default. Review the product demonstration and evaluate your own names, accent and apps within the 14-day refund window.
