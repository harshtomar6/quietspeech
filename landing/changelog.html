  • The listening overlay now shows in Notes, TextEdit and other apps, including apps in full-screen Spaces.

QuietSpeech 0.3.5

9 November 2026
  • Command-Tab app switching works again when Fn+Command is your Tap to Toggle shortcut.

  • Fn+Command starts dictation whichever key you press first.

QuietSpeech 0.3.4

9 November 2026
  • Tap to Toggle can now be a double-tap of your Hold to Talk key, or its own shortcut. Double-tap to start hands-free dictation, then tap once to stop.
  • Shortcuts that use modifier keys now save correctly, and picking a function key is easier.
  • Fixed freezes when dictation started, caused by macOS spell-checking.
  • Dictation no longer starts twice, and the microphone no longer turns on late after you let go of the shortcut.

QuietSpeech 0.3.3

28 October 2026
  • When a quick “at Priya” is heard as “add Priya”, QuietSpeech still types @Priya. A sentence like “Add Priya to the channel” stays as you said it.

QuietSpeech 0.3.2

27 October 2026
  • Say “at” before a name in a chat app, and QuietSpeech types it as an @mention, so “at Priya” becomes @Priya.

QuietSpeech 0.3.1

26 October 2026
  • A quick tap of Fn no longer opens the overlay. Hold the key to start dictating.

QuietSpeech 0.3

26 October 2026
  • Settings and the dictation overlay have a new Liquid Glass design.
  • The overlay shows one clear status at a time: a waveform while it listens, then progress, done or a warning.
  • QuietSpeech appears in the Dock and app switcher while one of its windows is open.

QuietSpeech 0.2

25 October 2026
  • QuietSpeech picks up names and terms from the window you're dictating into, so it spells them correctly.

QuietSpeech 0.1

25 October 2026

  • Dictation works in code editors such as VS Code and Cursor, and saying “dash dash” types --.
  • Generate fills in your name instead of leaving “[Your Name]”.
