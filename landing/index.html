Privacy

Your voice stays on this Mac by default.

Audio is transcribed on-device, and Apple Intelligence processes the text on-device. There is no account, no telemetry, and no audio kept after transcription.

  1. 01 Microphone Audio is captured only while you hold Fn.
  2. 02 On-device recognizer Apple’s local speech model turns it into text.
  3. 03 On-device cleanup Apple Intelligence removes filler and fixes punctuation.
  4. 04 Your app The finished text lands where your cursor is.

If you connect OpenAI, Groq, Ollama, or a custom endpoint, that provider sees what you send. QuietSpeech does not.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is there a trial?+

No. You buy a license once, and it works on two Macs. The demo above shows how it works before you pay. If it is not for you, you have 14 days to request a refund.

Does dictation work offline?+

By default, dictation and Apple Intelligence run on this Mac. If you connect another provider, audio goes there for transcription. The license check needs the internet once every 14 days, and the app keeps working inside that window.

Which apps does it work in?+

Anywhere you can type. Some apps need the clipboard fallback, and QuietSpeech handles that for you.

What happens if I get a new Mac?+

Deactivate the old Mac in the customer portal, then activate the new one with the same key.

Do I need an account?+

No. The license key is the account.

Does it work with Apple Intelligence turned off?+

Yes. Dictation still works, and cleanup falls back to on-device rules.

Why does it require macOS 26?+

The app uses Apple’s new Speech and Foundation Models frameworks. Both ship with macOS 26.
