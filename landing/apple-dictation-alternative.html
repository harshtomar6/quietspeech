First, check the built-in option

Use what fits your writing.

  1. Try Apple Dictation

    Open System Settings → Keyboard → Dictation. Enable it, choose your language and shortcut, then dictate a message in an app you use. If that meets your needs, keep using it.

  2. Decide what you want to add

    QuietSpeech is worth considering if you want its hold-to-talk workflow, live overlay, replacements or spoken editing. It will not necessarily fix recognition errors you already encounter with Apple’s engine.

  3. Set up a compatible Mac

    QuietSpeech needs Apple silicon and macOS 26+. After purchase, download the app, activate your license and grant Microphone and Accessibility permissions. Give built-in Dictation a different shortcut if both respond to Fn.

Before you buy

Know what changes.

Is QuietSpeech a different speech recognition engine?

The released default uses Apple’s on-device recognizer. QuietSpeech adds its own shortcut, overlay, cleanup, vocabulary and editing workflow around transcription. It also offers optional external providers.

Will it fix Apple Dictation recognition problems?

Not necessarily. Because the default recognizer is Apple’s, the same accent, language or microphone issues may remain. Before buying, check Apple’s troubleshooting guidance and your microphone and language settings.

Is my speech processed on my Mac?

By default, yes. Apple’s recognizer transcribes locally and Apple Intelligence handles local AI text work when available. Required speech assets may download during setup. Optional providers receive the selected audio or text when you choose to use them.

Is it a replacement for Voice Control?

QuietSpeech is designed for dictation and text work. If you need to navigate or operate your Mac by voice, look at Apple’s Voice Control. Insertion behavior in QuietSpeech can vary by app and text field.

Is there a trial?

There is no free trial. QuietSpeech costs $29 USD once for two Macs and has a 14-day refund policy. The license needs an internet check at least once every 14 days; the default dictation path can otherwise work offline after setup.
