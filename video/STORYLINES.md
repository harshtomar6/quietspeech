# QuietSpeech launch film: storylines

The one thing: you hold fn, talk, and finished text lands in the Mac app you're already in.
The thing to remember (in a viewer's words): "I just talk and it types it properly, wherever I am. And it's $29 once."

## A. One voice, four apps (picked)

- **Device / form:** one object across apps, carried by the capsule; one frame that rebuilds.
- **What you see:** the fn key turns into the capsule. Your words appear in large type, the filler drops out, and the sentence drops into a Notes window. The same window then rebuilds as Slack ("at Priya" becomes @Priya), Notes (spoken edit) and Mail (generate). The price and the mark close the film.
- **First 2 s:** a big fn keycap on a pale ground is pressed and widens into the QuietSpeech capsule; "Hi Priya," appears above it as it's heard.
- **Beats:** fn → spoken words → filler gone, inserted → "Say it, and it's written." → @mention → edit → generate → on this Mac → $29 once → mark.
- **Look:** pale #E9EBF1 and ink #0A0C10 worlds, Inter, the app's dark glass capsule as the one material, cyan only for what's live.
- **Sound:** soft key click on fn, a tick when text lands, a whoosh on world changes; no music bed is available locally (see SPEC).

Why it's recommended: every second shows the product doing the one thing, and the capsule gives the eye one carrier.

## B. The spoken word is the picture

- Giant kinetic type of what you say fills the frame; each sentence collapses into a real text field at the end. Bolder and more typographic, but less product context.

## C. A morning of messages

- A moment in someone's day: 9:41, an invoice to chase, a Slack thread, a reply. One person clears three messages without touching the keyboard. Warmer, but it needs more set dressing than real UI can carry.
