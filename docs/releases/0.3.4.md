# QuietSpeech 0.3.4

- Choose double-tapping your Hold to Talk key or a separate shortcut for Tap to Toggle. In double-tap mode, tap twice to start hands-free dictation and once to stop; holding still works.
- Fix recording and saving modifier-key shortcuts, including Right Option, and improve function-key selection.
- Fix dictation startup freezes caused by waiting for macOS spell-checking.
- Prevent overlapping dictation startups and late microphone starts after releasing the shortcut.
