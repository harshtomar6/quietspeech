# QuietSpeech 0.1

Released: 2026-10-25

- First public release. Hold a key, speak, and QuietSpeech types your words into the Mac app you're using. Speech is transcribed on your Mac.
- AI cleanup leaves short dictations as they are, and it edits your words instead of replying to them.
- Dictation works in code editors such as VS Code and Cursor, and saying “dash dash” types --.
- Generate fills in your name instead of leaving “[Your Name]”.
