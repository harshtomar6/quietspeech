# QuietSpeech — audience and positioning

Reviewed: 2026-10-26. The primary audience and promise below were set by the owner. Other segments are inferred from site examples and remain hypotheses.

## Primary audience

Mac users who want a dictation app without a monthly payment. The owner’s intended promise is **fast, easy, private dictation for Mac with no recurring subscription**. The no-subscription offer is shown on the site; “fast” and “easy” are intended experience qualities, not measured comparative claims.

## Candidate segments

| Segment | Job QuietSpeech may help with | Evidence in repository |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Developers who dictate around code | Dictate comments or commands while preserving syntax and shell flags. | [README](../README.md), [context profiles](../Sources/QuietSpeech/ContextProfile.swift) |
| Privacy-conscious Mac users | Use on-device transcription and cleanup by default, with a choice of external providers. | [README](../README.md), [site privacy section](../landing/index.html) |
| Mac users who write frequent messages and notes | Get spoken thoughts into Slack, Mail, Notes, and documents without switching apps. | [Site demos](../landing/index.html) |

## Positioning currently expressed on the site

- Main promise: “Say it, and it’s written.”
- Supporting description: dictate, edit, and generate text in any Mac app; on-device by default.
- Offer: buy once for two Macs, without an account or subscription.

The owner’s audience and promise above set the current direction; the site lines are existing published copy. Customer response has not yet been measured in this memory. See [marketing](marketing.md) for channel and claim guidance, and [product](product.md) for exact behavior.
